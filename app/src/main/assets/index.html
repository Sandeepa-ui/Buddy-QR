How it works

1 Design the card

Upload your sample card PNG in Card Configurations — it becomes the card face on the 3.3 × 2.1 inch canvas — or pick one of the built-in sample templates.

2 Enroll & issue

Enroll buddies onto your cards — 16-digit ID and expiry generate automatically; the QR rides on the back and exports as pure code or a 300 DPI card PNG.

3 Scan & provision

Scan to open live details: mark loans Given/Completed, give gifts, record leaves and salary advances — every card condition enforced automatically.
